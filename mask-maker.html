Using the mask in OBS

  1. Right-click your camera source → Filters → under Effect Filters click + → Image Mask/Blend.
  2. Type: Alpha Mask (Alpha Channel). Path: choose the PNG you downloaded.
  3. Optional: add a Crop/Pad filter to trim the empty sides, then drag frame.html on top for the themed ring.
