BACKUPS
=======
The Show Runner's "Save backup file" button saves backups into this folder
(awym-backup-YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM.json). Each one holds every dock's settings:
people, background title, stream screen words, theme, stats options,
Content Panel pieces (with photos), rundown, scoreboard, receipts, your own
sounds, clips, moments and guest seats. Never your OBS password.

To restore: Show Runner → Backup & restore → "Open backup file…" and pick
one of these, then click Restore twice and restart OBS.

Saving here needs the setup script (awym-obs-setup.lua) loaded in
Tools → Scripts and the Show Runner connected to OBS (Clips & replays).
