YOUR OWN SOUNDS
===============
Any sound on the sound board can be swapped for a real recording: put an audio
file in THIS folder, named after the sound, and reload the Sound Effects source
(in OBS: right-click it → Refresh) and the Show Runner dock. Its pad then says
YOURS.

  airhorn      Air horn            ooh          Crowd ooh
  buzzer       Buzzer              applause     Applause
  ding         Correct             chaching     Cha-ching
  wrong        Wrong               sadtrombone  Sad trombone
  rimshot      Rimshot             scratch      Record scratch
  drumroll     Drumroll            swoosh       Swoosh
  whistle      Whistle             gong         Gong
  boing        Boing               micdrop      Mic drop
  tinfoil      Tin Foil sound

  File types: .mp3, .ogg, .wav, .m4a or .webm, e.g.  sounds/airhorn.mp3
  Delete the file to go back to the kit's sound.

Which sound plays: your file here → the kit's recording (sounds/kit/, real
recordings of applause, cha-ching, correct, wrong, swoosh, boing and mic drop)
→ the version made in code.

TIPS
  - Keep them short (most under 3 seconds) and trim silence off the start, so
    they land right when you press the key.
  - Match the loudness roughly to the other pads; the board's volume slider
    and the Sound Effects slider in OBS's Audio Mixer still apply.
  - Use sounds you're allowed to use on a stream. Sources that allow it:
      Pixabay sound effects (pixabay.com/sound-effects): free for streams and
        videos, no credit needed. Search "air horn", "rimshot", "sad trombone",
        "drum roll", "crowd ooh", "referee whistle", "gong", "record scratch".
      Freesound (freesound.org): filter the licence to "Creative Commons 0"
        for sounds you can use with no credit. CC BY sounds are fine too, but
        then credit the author (e.g. in your video description).
    Avoid ripping sounds from TV broadcasts, games or songs: those can get a
    stream or video muted or struck.
