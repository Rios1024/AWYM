Pick each person's mic (remote guests: their AWYM Guest Seat source). The bar shows their level; the yellow line is the Sensitivity. A person is talking when their bar crosses it. Mics in the same room hear each other: Ignore mic bleed skips a mic that's this many dB quieter than the loudest one, so only real talking over each other counts. Backup & restore

Saves everything from every dock in one file: people, background title, stream screen words, theme, stats options, Content Panel pieces (with photos), rundown, scoreboard, receipts, your sounds, clips and moments. Your OBS password is never included.

Inside OBS, Save backup file puts the file in the kit's backups folder. It needs the setup script loaded (Tools → Scripts) and this dock connected to OBS (Clips & replays above). Without that, the backup is copied for you to paste into Notepad.
