Remote guests

Bring people in over the internet with VDO.Ninja (free, works in Chrome or Edge, nothing to install). Each remote guest becomes their own source in OBS, so they get a camera frame, a lower third and the ★ spotlight like anyone in the room.

1. Your room

Keep these the same every week and the links never change. The password keeps strangers out: anyone with it could watch, so only share invite links, never this page.

2. Who sits in each seat seats = People slots 1–6 in the Control Panel

Works when this page is open as a dock in OBS. Otherwise, type the names in the Control Panel's People section.

Saved guests people who come back: their name, role and badge picture

Fill in a seat (name, role, badge), then Save as guest. Next time, pick them from the seat's Load a saved guest list, or with Seat… here, and click Send names and badges. Saving someone with the same name again updates them.

3. Your link join the room so your guests can hear and see you

  • Open Your link in Chrome or Edge on the computer running OBS, pick your mic (and camera if you want guests to see you), and leave the tab open during the show.
  • Wear headphones. In OBS, turn off Desktop Audio (Settings → Audio → Desktop Audio: Disabled) so the guests aren't recorded twice: their sound already comes from their own sources.
  • The director link (optional) lets you mute guests, check their connection and change settings from one page.

4. Let the setup script add the guests optional

In OBS: Tools → Scripts → awym-obs-setup.lua, fill in these three boxes, then click Build my scenes. It puts each remote guest behind their camera frame in Main Show, already cropped and cut out to match. (If Main Show already exists, tick "add the overlays it's missing".)

Before the first show

  • Do a 5-minute test call with each guest: Chrome or Edge on a computer, headphones, a light in front of them (not behind), and a wired connection if they can.
  • Phones work too: they open the invite link in the phone's browser and allow camera and mic.
  • In OBS, each guest source has Control audio via OBS ticked, so every guest gets their own slider in the Audio Mixer.
  • If a guest's picture freezes, right-click their source → Refresh. If their sound echoes, someone isn't on headphones.
